  Purva Orient Grand, Puravankara's single-tower residence on Lal Bagh Main Road at Sudhama Nagar, is in the final stages of construction. The project was registered with Karnataka RERA on 7 September 2021, opened for sales in January 2022, and carries a RERA-filed completion date of 30 June 2027. The occupancy certificate has not yet been received.

  That last point matters more than it sounds, because some listing portals describe the project as "ready to occupy". That is incorrect. Purva Orient Grand is an under-construction project nearing completion. A buyer who plans a move, a lease exit or a loan around a portal label rather than the RERA record will be planning around the wrong date.

  ## Where the project stands

  The fundamentals are on record. Purva Orient Grand is a single tower of two basements, ground and twenty-four floors on 1.15 acres, with 97 homes in total: 3 BHK and 4 BHK residences across four grades. No residence shares a common wall with another, and every home has private decks. The twenty-fourth and twenty-fifth levels are given over to The Rose & Cypress, a Victorian-inspired rooftop club designed by Andy Fisher of Singapore, with an infinity pool at the twenty-fifth level. The project belongs to the WorldHome Collection, Puravankara's ultra-luxury line.

  ## What "final stages" means if you are buying now

  A project in its final stages of construction is not yet complete. For a buyer today, that means four things.

  **You are buying a building, not a render.** Buyers at the January 2022 launch committed to drawings. Today the tower stands on Lal Bagh Main Road, and a site visit lets you judge the approach, the neighbourhood and the setting beside Lalbagh Botanical Garden for yourself. We arrange site visits, including the club level where access permits.

  **The timeline is shorter, but 30 June 2027 is a filed completion date, not a handover date.** Possession can be offered only after the occupancy certificate is issued. Plan a move, a lease exit or a loan schedule around the RERA record and your agreement to sell.

  **Tax treatment follows construction status.** Because the occupancy certificate has not been received, GST at 5% applies to the under-construction consideration, on top of stamp duty of approximately 5% to 6% plus cess and registration at 1%. The [price page](/price) lists every additional charge.

  **Inventory is thin.** Ninety-seven homes, one tower and no next phase. Puravankara's current published position is "₹9 crore onwards", which indicates that the larger and higher-floor residences are what remain. Read our [inventory update](/news/purva-orient-grand-limited-inventory-9-crore-onwards-2026) before you shortlist a grade.

  ## Your money under RERA: the escrow rule

  Purva Orient Grand is registered with Karnataka RERA under PRM/KA/RERA/1251/310/PR/210907/004299. Under Section 4(2)(l)(D) of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016, seventy per cent of the amounts collected from buyers must be deposited in a separate account and used only for the construction and land cost of the project. For a buyer committing late in the build, that ring-fencing is the statutory assurance that buyers' money is spent on this building and nowhere else.

  Verify the registration yourself at [rera.karnataka.gov.in](https://rera.karnataka.gov.in) before you book. The Act also requires promoters to publish a quarterly status of the project on the regulator's website, so the RERA project page is the right place to track progress against the filed date.

  ## Built with MIVAN: why the structure matters

  The superstructure is MIVAN aluminium-formwork RCC, seismic compliant, with solid concrete block masonry: 200 mm external walls and 100 mm internal walls. MIVAN produces a monolithic concrete structure with far tighter dimensional tolerances than conventional block-and-plaster construction. In practice, that means flatter walls, fewer cracks over time and better acoustic separation between spaces. It costs more to build, and it is one of the reasons the project is priced where it is.
